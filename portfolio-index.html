PUBLISHED STUDY / SQL & PYTHON

Retail sales, explained.

Where does invoice value come from, and what changes after accounting for credits and data quality? This study analyzes 541,909 original invoice lines from a UK online retailer.

    What the analysis reveals

    • The UK contributes 84.01% of net invoice value, making the domestic market the largest exposure.
    • November 2011 is the highest full month: £1,456,145.80 in net invoice value. Investigate capacity and stock planning ahead of the late-year peak.
    • REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER leads merchandise-like SKUs: £164,459.49 in net invoice value. Profitability cannot be assessed without cost data.

    These are descriptive findings and suggested follow-up questions. No business intervention or causal impact is claimed.

    METHOD & LIMITATIONS

    Decisions a reviewer can trace

    • Remove 5,268 exact repeated rows under an explicit duplicate policy. Retain the original workbook and report the alternative total.
    • Exclude 2,512 nonpositive-price rows after deduplication. Retain negative quantities as credit or adjustment lines.
    • Keep missing customer IDs in financial totals. Avoid customer-level conclusions that require identifying those customers.
    • Round extended line amounts to integer pence. Flag the partial final month before comparing trends.
    Duplicate-policy sensitivity: keeping otherwise eligible repeated rows yields £9,769,872.05 in net invoice value, £21,740.98 above the chosen total. Identical invoice lines can represent legitimate repetition, so this policy requires business confirmation.
    Read the SQL for monthly comparisons
    WITH monthly AS (
  SELECT month,
    SUM(CASE WHEN line_type = 'sale'
        THEN line_value_pence ELSE 0 END) AS gross_pence,
    SUM(line_value_pence) AS net_pence
  FROM invoice_lines
  GROUP BY month
)
SELECT *, LAG(gross_pence) OVER (ORDER BY month)
  AS previous_gross_pence
FROM monthly ORDER BY month;

    This excerpt shows the aggregation and window function. The complete query and the partial-month handling are included in the project download.
